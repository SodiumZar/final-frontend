## About UK-Guard

UK-Guard is a comprehensive campus reporting system designed specifically for Universitas Klabat. This platform empowers students to report facility issues, security concerns, academic problems, and environmental issues in real-time. 

Our mission is to create a safer, more responsive campus environment by streamlining the reporting process and ensuring that student concerns are heard and addressed promptly. Through UK-Guard, students can easily submit reports with photos, track the status of their submissions, and stay informed about campus improvements—all while fostering a culture of community engagement and accountability.
